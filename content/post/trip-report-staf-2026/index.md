The STAF conference(s) are a quite unique place among all types of software related conferences around. It is a federated conference with many different co-located events ocurring in paralllel, including conferences, workshops and contests. This is not absolutely different, since many other conferences also offer support for co-located events, but they normally share a common topic and reach (almost) the same community, while STAF allows the connection between related, but communities that not so often are in the same place.

In 2026, the event occured in teh city of Rennes (France) between June 30th and July 3rd. In 2026, it's interesting to see this mixture of communities (and views) around software development and language engineering
